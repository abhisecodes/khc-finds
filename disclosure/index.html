Amazon Associates Program Statement

KHC Finds is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for websites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.in.

What This Means for You as a Reader

When you click on product links in our Products catalog or within our Journal buying guides and make a qualifying purchase on Amazon India:

  • Zero Extra Cost to You: You pay the exact same price as any other Amazon shopper—including festive discounts, lightning deals, and bank coupons.
  • Referral Credit: Amazon India pays KHC Finds a small referral fee (typically between 1% and 9% depending on the merchandise category) for directing a purchase to their marketplace.

Our Commitment to Editorial Integrity

Our affiliate participation strictly does not compromise our editorial judgment:

  1. No Pay-to-Play Placements: Brands cannot pay for favorable inclusion in our curated selections or buying guides.
  2. Objective Evaluation: Every product includes explicit considerations and drawbacks alongside its merits. If a product fails our build quality or ergonomics threshold, we do not recommend it.
  3. Price Accuracy: We verify all listed prices, MRPs, and discount tags against live Amazon India data. We never artificially inflate historical MRPs to exaggerate discount percentages.

Price and Availability Fluctuations

Product prices and availability on Amazon India fluctuate frequently due to dynamic festive sales, lightning deals, seller inventory changes, and bank coupon offers. While all prices, MRPs, and discount tags featured on KHC Finds are verified against official data at the time of publication, the exact price and availability information displayed on Amazon.in at the time of purchase will apply to the purchase of any product.
