About KHC Finds

Objects Engineered for Clarity

We cut through retail marketing noise to curate functional, beautifully built tools and electronics worth owning.

Who We Are

KHC Finds is an independent digital publication and product curation desk based in India. We operate at the intersection of industrial design, home office ergonomics, consumer electronics, and daily carry utility.

The consumer internet is flooded with sponsored listicles, AI-generated generic catalogs, and misleading discount percentages. We founded KHC Finds on a single principle: to only recommend objects we have vetted through official technical documentation, real price verification, and genuine functional merit.

Our Curation Principles

1. Utility Over Novelty

We do not feature gimmicks. Every television, curved monitor, keyboard, or travel backpack highlighted on KHC Finds must solve a concrete physical problem—whether that is reducing ocular strain during marathon desk sessions, ensuring 7-year software longevity, or passing airport security without opening your main luggage.

2. Verified Price Tracking

We monitor live pricing and verified price drops on Amazon India. When we list an item as discounted, we cross-reference manufacturer MRPs and historical pricing trends rather than trusting artificial price spikes.

3. Transparent Editorial Independence

We participate in the Amazon India Associates program. Our affiliate relationships never influence which products we recommend or how we critique them. If a product has a flaw—such as a 60Hz refresh limit on an otherwise excellent television or standard zippers on an economical travel bag—we explicitly document it in our considerations.
