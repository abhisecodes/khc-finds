The 43-inch television category has fundamentally evolved for 2026. Once treated by major manufacturers as an entry-level compromise for bedrooms and compact living rooms, the 108–109 cm bracket is now a battleground for premium panel technologies. Flagship Mini-LED backlighting, Quantum Dot (QLED) wide color gamuts, and high screen refresh rates (120Hz HSR) are now readily accessible under ₹35,000 during Amazon festive sales.

In this guide, we break down three of the strongest contenders in this size class: the Samsung 43-inch Mini LED (UA43M2EHAULXL), the Philips 8100 Series 4K QLED (43PQT8100/94), and the LG NU87 AI Series NanoCell (43NU870BPLA).

1. Samsung UA43M2EHAULXL: The Mini-LED Contrast Benchmark

Samsung’s 2026 entry is arguably the most ambitious 43-inch screen launched this cycle. By replacing conventional edge LED lighting with a dense array of miniature LEDs, the UA43M2EHAULXL achieves deep black levels that standard IPS and VA panels cannot replicate without severe halo blooming.

Beyond panel contrast, Samsung has addressed the single biggest criticism of smart televisions: software obsolescence. Samsung guarantees 7 years of Tizen OS upgrades for this model, pairing it with their neural 4K Upscaling processor and 150+ free streaming channels via Samsung TV Plus.

The Final Verdict

Each of these displays delivers genuine value depending on your primary use-case:

  • Choose the Samsung Mini-LED if you watch primarily in darkened rooms, stream movies, and prioritize longevity with guaranteed 7-year OS updates.
  • Choose the Philips QLED if you play fast-paced video games, follow live sports, or desire maximum color saturation and Dolby Vision support out of the box.
  • Choose the LG NanoCell if you require lifelike, non-fatiguing natural colors, advanced smart-home integrations, or virtual 9.1.2 surround up-mixing.
